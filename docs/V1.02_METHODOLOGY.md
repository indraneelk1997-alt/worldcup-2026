# V1.02 Methodology

A forward-looking explanation of what V1.02 does and why. Companion to
`KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` (which catalogs what V1.02 does NOT do and why).

V1.02 was tagged at commit `29601b9` on 2026-05-21. See git tag `v1.02`.

---

## Goal

V1.02's goal was to fix the two worst limitations of V1.01:

1. **Small-sample noise in player ratings** — V1.01's `rating_per_90` was a
   raw per-90 metric. Players with low minutes (just above the 450-min
   floor) could post inflated values from a hot streak. Joshua Zirkzee
   appearing at #2 in 2025-26 with only 560 minutes was the canonical
   failure case.
2. **Unrealistic starting XIs** — V1.01 picked the top-11 players by
   `rating_per_90` regardless of position. This produced XIs with 0
   goalkeepers, 0 defenders, and 11 attackers. Predictions ran on
   imaginary lineups.

V1.02 addresses both, plus introduces a scenario-based schema that
unblocks V1.03+ extensions (multi-scenario predictions, formation-aware
modeling, opponent-specific lineups).

```

    [Hungarian assignment over             reads player_season_stats +
     player_positions × formation_slots    player_positions + formations
```

## Component 1: Data ingest (carried from V1.01)

Per-season player stats from Understat via `soccerdata` are filtered to
≥450-minute EPL players for the two most recent seasons (2024-2025,
2025-2026). Loaded into `player_season_stats` with primary key
(player_id, season, team). The 450-minute floor is V1.01's lever for
trimming noise; V1.02 layers shrinkage on top rather than replacing it.

## Component 2: Bayesian shrinkage (S7)

**The formula:**
```
shrunk_value = w × observed_value + (1 - w) × prior
where w = minutes / (minutes + k)
```

**Two shrunk columns are stored per row:**
- `shrunk_form` — per-row shrinkage of `rating_per_90` using that row's
  own minutes. Captures recent form.
- `shrunk_consistency` — career minutes-weighted aggregate across all
  seasons for that player_id, then shrunk. Captures stable ability.

**Calibration values:**
- `prior = 0.2390` — the minutes-weighted league mean of all ≥450-min
  rows. Three candidate priors were computed (see KNOWN_LIMITATIONS);
  spread across the three is 0.0037, so the choice barely matters.
- `k = 900` — heuristic. Represents "effective minutes of the prior" —
  the minutes count at which a player is 50% observed, 50% prior.
  10 full matches = 900 minutes. Not derived from data; empirical Bayes
  alternative was deferred to V1.03 (gated on per-match data).

**Verification:**
- Zirkzee 560 min, raw 0.814 → shrunk_form 0.459 — falls out of Top 10.
- Haaland 0.944 → 0.781 (barely moves due to high minutes).
- Salah's form-vs-consistency gap of -0.262 correctly flags a sub-baseline
  2025-26 season.

The simulator blends the two columns as
`0.75 × shrunk_form + 0.25 × shrunk_consistency` via a `form_weight`
parameter logged on every prediction row.

## Component 3: Realistic XI selection (S8)

The biggest V1.02 piece. Replaces V1.01's "top-11 by rating" with a
formation-aware, multi-position-eligibility-aware assignment.

### Sub-component 3a: Multi-position data layer

Understat's `position` field is a space-separated string of single-letter
codes (e.g. 'D M S' means defender who has also played midfield, where
'S' marks substitute appearances). About 30% of EPL players in 2025-2026
have multi-class eligibility.

Two parallel stores:
- `player_season_stats.position_class` — primary class (first non-S token).
- `player_positions` table — one row per (player, season, team,
  position_class) with a `priority` field (1 = primary, 2+ = secondary).

A consistency invariant is enforced at backfill time: the priority=1 row
in `player_positions` must match `position_class` for every season row.

### Sub-component 3b: Formation library

10 formations seeded with their 11 slot definitions each:

| Formation | Class breakdown |
|---|---|
| 4-3-3   | GK=1 DEF=4 MID=3 FWD=3 |
| 4-2-3-1 | GK=1 DEF=4 MID=5 FWD=1 |
| 4-4-2   | GK=1 DEF=4 MID=4 FWD=2 |
| 4-1-4-1 | GK=1 DEF=4 MID=5 FWD=1 |
| 4-5-1   | GK=1 DEF=4 MID=5 FWD=1 |
| 3-4-3   | GK=1 DEF=5 MID=2 FWD=3 |
| 3-5-2   | GK=1 DEF=5 MID=3 FWD=2 |
| 3-4-2-1 | GK=1 DEF=5 MID=3 FWD=2 |
| 5-3-2   | GK=1 DEF=5 MID=3 FWD=2 |
| 5-4-1   | GK=1 DEF=5 MID=4 FWD=1 |

Wing-backs (LWB/RWB) in all back-3 / back-5 formations are classified as
DEF, not MID. See KNOWN_LIMITATIONS for the structural-vs-functional
classification rationale and the V1.03 plan to resolve this with
per-match data.

### Sub-component 3c: Hungarian assignment

For each (team, formation) pair, build a cost matrix where rows are the
team's eligible outfielders and columns are the formation's 10 outfielder
slots. Cell value is `-(blended_rating × multiplier × bonus)` for eligible
pairs, `+INELIGIBLE_COST (1e9)` otherwise. Solve with
`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment` — Hungarian algorithm, O(n³).

The solver picks 10 distinct players, one per slot, maximizing total
score. GK is locked separately by most-minutes; same player chosen
regardless of formation.

For each team, all 10 formations are evaluated; the top 3 by total
`selection_score` are stored in `best_xi`. Result: 20 teams × 3
formations × 11 slots = 660 rows.

### Sub-component 3d: Selection scoring

```
selection_score = blended_rating × class_multiplier × slot_bonus
```

**Class multipliers** (S8 tuned):
- GK = locked-in by minutes (not in cost matrix)
- DEF = 2.0
- MID = 1.25
- FWD = 1.0

The rationale: defenders post lower `rating_per_90` than forwards
because the metric is offensive (npxG+xA). The multipliers compensate so
defenders can win their slots. Started at 1/2/4 (catastrophic — 7
defenders per team), tuned through 1/1.5/2 (MID-heavy), landed at
1/1.25/2. Final state is robust to multipliers — formations do the heavy
lifting.

**Hybrid slot bonus** (1.1x):
Applied when a slot's position_code is in {DM, CAM, LWB, RWB} AND the
player has the matching multi-class eligibility:
- DM, LWB, RWB → player must have BOTH DEF and MID eligibility
- CAM → player must have BOTH FWD and MID eligibility

Rewards hybrid players in hybrid roles. A pure DM gets no bonus; a
DEF+MID-eligible player gets 1.1x.

### Output and validation

660 rows in `best_xi`. Validation:
- 13/20 teams' #1 fit is 4-2-3-1 (modern EPL norm)
- 6/20 teams' #1 fit is 3-4-2-1 (Arteta/Maresca style)
- 1/20 (Everton) is 4-4-2
- Class distribution across rank=1 XIs: GK=20, DEF=86, MID=87, FWD=27

This is football-realistic and a vast improvement over V1.01's 0-DEF,
0-GK XIs.

## Component 4: Match prediction (S9)

V1.02 simulator at `src/simulate/poisson_sim_v102.py`. Inherits V1.01's
linear-differential xG + Poisson sampling, but with two important
changes:

1. **Schema-aware** — reads lineups via `scenario_id` (not `fixture_id`
   directly), per the S6 schema refactor.
2. **Shrunk + blended ratings** — uses
   `0.75 × shrunk_form + 0.25 × shrunk_consistency` instead of raw
   `rating_per_90`.
3. **Home advantage** — new `HOME_BONUS = 0.15` term (home gets +0.30
   net goals/match), empirically supported in football analytics.
4. **Two variants** — see "The two variants" section below.

### The two variants

V1.02 ships TWO simulator variants per fixture:

**`v1.02_unweighted`** (primary):
```
strength = SUM(0.75 × shrunk_form + 0.25 × shrunk_consistency)
```
Across all 11 XI players.

**`v1.02_weighted`** (diagnostic):
```
strength = SUM(blended_rating × OFFENSIVE_WEIGHT[position_code])
```
Where `OFFENSIVE_WEIGHT` is an 18-code table with class anchors derived
from data (DEF/FWD ratio = 0.29, MID/FWD ratio = 0.54 from 2025-2026
class means) and within-class football-intuition tuning (CB < FB < WB,
DM < CM < CAM, ST ≈ wide forwards).

**Why both?**
The user (and later the data) identified that `rating_per_90` already
encodes positional effects — applying explicit position weights on top
double-counts. The weighted variant systematically pulled predictions
toward equality (Sunderland-Chelsea: 86% Chelsea win unweighted → 48%
weighted, contradicting real-world standings).

The unweighted variant is the V1.02 primary model. The weighted variant
is kept as a diagnostic for retrospective comparison once MD38 actuals
land. V1.03 will redesign position weighting (see KNOWN_LIMITATIONS).

### xG formula

```
xG_home = max(0, BASE_GOALS + HOME_BONUS + K × (strength_home - strength_away))
xG_away = max(0, BASE_GOALS - HOME_BONUS + K × (strength_away - strength_home))
```

Linear-differential, inherited from V1.01. Known limitation: floors away
xG at zero when home strength dominates (Aston Villa anomaly — see
KNOWN_LIMITATIONS). V1.03+ should fix.

### Poisson sampling

`numpy.random.default_rng(seed=42).poisson(xg, size=10_000)` for each
side. Independent — no Dixon-Coles correction yet.

## Known limitations

See `KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md` for the full catalog of design decisions and
their caveats. The V1.02 section has 10 sub-sections covering: heuristic
shrinkage, league-mean prior, form-vs-consistency weighting, selection
multipliers, wing-back classification, multi-position handling, best_xi
standalone-ness, formation library scope, GK contribution to total score,
two-variant policy, and linear-xG breakdown at extreme strength gaps.

## Open questions for V1.03+

These are the immediate next steps V1.02 surfaced. The full V1.03 task
list lives in the Notion tracker; this is the methodology-doc-level
summary.

1. **Per-match data ingest** — unlocks empirical Bayes, per-position
   priors, finer position vocabulary, form trajectories. Recon completed
   in S10; data is rich (~22K rows × 19 cols including per-match xG,
   xA, position).
2. **Empirical Bayes shrinkage** — replaces heuristic `k=900` once
   per-match data is in.
3. **Linear-xG formula fix** — saturating function or strength
   normalization for the Aston Villa anomaly.
4. **Position weighting redesign** — fix the V1.02 double-counting
   compression, possibly by applying weights to selection only (already
   done in S8), OR by position-conditional team strength rather than
   per-player multiplicative.
5. **Opponent Elo** — ClubElo CSV is publicly accessible (recon
   completed in S10). Cheap integration.
6. **Defensive team strength** — using opponent xG against you as the
   modern xGA proxy, from the per-team-match data ingested.
7. **Dixon-Coles correction** — fixes Poisson independence assumption
   for low-scoring outcomes.
8. **Substitutes in simulation** — currently only the starting XI's
   blended ratings drive strength.
9. **Predicted opponent XI** — currently rank-1 best_xi is locked; could
   vary the XI by opponent.
10. **V1.02 retrospective** — Brier scores / log loss comparing both
    variants to MD38 actuals once results land (May 24, 2026).
